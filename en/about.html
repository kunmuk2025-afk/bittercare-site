Product concept

At the spot they chew,
apply with precision.

BitterCare is a supporting behavior-care sticker that adds bitter taste to frequently mouthed household surfaces. Apply it directly to the problem area instead of spraying widely. Made with a premium UK-sourced bitter ingredient.

Macfarlan Smith · Veranova UK

The routine is simple.

A supporting tool for finding the right spot and observing, rather than a complicated training device.

1

Find the spot

Identify the furniture, door frame or cord area your dog's mouth actually touches.

2

Cut what you need

Cut slightly larger than the problem area.

3

Press every edge down

Clean and fully dry the surface, then apply firmly at the contact point.

4

Watch the response

Observe for the first few days, adjust placement or replace if needed.
