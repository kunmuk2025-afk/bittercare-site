Premium UK-sourced bitter ingredient

A signal through taste,
rather than a masking scent.

BitterCare adds bitter taste to household surfaces to help manage repeated mouthing.

🇬🇧

What is the ingredient?

The bitter ingredient is denatonium benzoate. BitterCare uses a premium UK-sourced bitter ingredient.

🍃

What does the bitter taste do?

It allows a bitter-taste response when the dog naturally contacts the treated surface. Placement and close adhesion matter.

🐾

Will every dog respond the same way?

Taste sensitivity and habits differ. Stop use if the dog tears the sticker, and consider alternative chewing and environmental management.

Check both the source and the instructions.

“UK-sourced” refers to the origin of the bitter ingredient, not the manufacturing location of the finished product. It is not edible. Do not offer it to taste or apply to the body.

More about the bitter ingredient

Denatonium benzoate has an extremely bitter taste. General ingredient information is available in Bitrex's ingredient guide. This is not a certification or guarantee of BitterCare's finished product.

Not every dog responds the same way.

Response depends on taste sensitivity, chewing habits, contact point and environment. If the response seems weak, check placement and adhesion first. Never make your dog taste it directly.

Safety FAQ

Common questions before the first use.

Can I apply it to the body or fur?

No. Do not use on skin, fur or paw pads. This product is for household surfaces only.

Can I let my dog taste it directly?

Do not offer it directly to taste. Apply it to frequently chewed surfaces so contact occurs naturally.

Can I use it on delicate furniture?

Adhesive marks or surface changes are possible depending on the material. Test a small area first.

What if the effect gets weaker?

Check the contact point and adhesion. Replace the sticker if surface dirt or repeated use makes replacement necessary.

Manage electrical safety separately.

BitterCare is not an insulation or electric-shock protection product. Do not use on damaged cords. Keep cords out of reach and unplug before applying.
