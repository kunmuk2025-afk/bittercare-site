PERSONALITY & CHEWING

Even when chewing,
your dog's needs can offer clues.

Exploring with the mouth is natural for dogs. Curiosity or boredom may contribute to chewing. Looking at the objects, places and situations involved can help you find an approach that suits your dog.

  • Provide suitable chew toys and enjoyable playtime.
  • Keep simple notes about when and what your dog chews.
  • Consult a professional if behavior changes suddenly or health concerns arise.

FAQ

Questions about the personality check

Is the personality check free?

Yes. It is free, with no product purchase required.

Does it replace a behavioral diagnosis?

No. It provides reference information based on your observations. For health or behavior concerns, consult a veterinarian or behavior professional.

Can puppies take the check?

Yes, but behavior may change with growth and environment. Answer based on recent observations and check again later.

Can the result change?

Results may vary with the situations you recall or recent changes. Use them as a starting point for ongoing observation, not a fixed conclusion.
