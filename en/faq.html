Response and use

Useful before and during use.

Does every dog respond the same way?

No. Response varies with taste sensitivity, habit strength, contact point and environment.

Where should I apply it?

Apply precisely where your dog's mouth makes contact, not just nearby.

How long should I observe at first?

Observe over the first few days and adjust placement and coverage. If the response is weak, check adhesion and the actual contact point first.

Can I let my dog taste it directly?

Do not offer directly to taste. Apply to repeatedly chewed household surfaces for natural contact.

Can it be used on cords?

Check for damage and unplug first, then wrap snugly in short sections. This is not electrical insulation or shock protection. Never use on damaged cords, and keep cords out of reach.

What if the effect seems weaker?

Check dirt, contact frequency and placement. Replace the sticker as needed.

Will removal leave marks?

This depends on the material. Test delicate surfaces first, and peel slowly from a corner when removing.

What if my dog chews the sticker itself?

Check for lifted edges and coverage. If tearing continues, remove the sticker and offer a suitable chew item.

Can I use it on a sofa or rug?

Fabric, rugs and rough surfaces may not adhere well. Use mainly on smooth surfaces and test delicate materials first.

Is purchase required for the free analysis?

No purchase is needed to use the free personality analysis. Products are sold separately and results are for reference only.
