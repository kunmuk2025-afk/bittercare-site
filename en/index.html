More questions ↗
Will chewing stop immediately?

Taste sensitivity and habits vary by dog. Check the actual contact point and adhesion, and supervise the first use.

My dog tries to tear off the sticker.

Check for lifted edges and sufficient coverage. If chewing the sticker continues, remove it and offer an appropriate chew item.

Can I use it on a sofa or rug?

Fabric, rugs and rough surfaces may not hold well. Use smooth household surfaces and test delicate materials on a small area first.

Do I need to buy the product to take the check?

You can use the free personality check before buying. The check is free; BitterCare products are sold separately.
