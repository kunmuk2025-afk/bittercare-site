More questions ↗
Do all pets respond the same way?

Responses may vary with temperament and sensory sensitivity. Reactions to bitterness and the time needed to adjust can differ, so please supervise the first use.

How long can I use it?

Duration varies with temperature, humidity, friction and adhesion. Remove and replace the sticker if the response decreases or it becomes damaged or lifts.

Where can I apply it?

Use on flat, dry, smooth contact areas such as furniture and door frames. Uneven or wet surfaces, fabric and rugs may not adhere well. Check suitability and adhesion first on other materials, including cable covers.

What should I know about pet safety?

BitterCare is a supporting care sticker that adds bitterness to contact areas. Do not let your pet chew or swallow the sticker. Remove it if biting or tearing continues, and stop use if an unusual reaction occurs. See Before Use for ingredients and detailed guidance.

Can removal leave marks?

This depends on the surface material and finish. Test a small hidden area first on delicate materials such as fine wood or special finishes.
