Usage guide

BitterCare,
here's how to use it.

Cover the full contact area with a little extra, and keep edges flat. Placement and adhesion matter.

The basics Peeling the backing Practical guide Pre-use checklist

Four steps for the first day.

Clean, cut, apply and observe. Follow them in order.

1

Clean the surface

Remove dust and oil, then dry completely to help the adhesive hold.

2

Cut to size

Cut slightly larger than the area actually chewed.

3

Apply at the contact point

Remove the backing and press firmly onto the actual contact area with no lifted edges.

4

Observe the response

Observe over the first few days, adjust placement and replace with a new sticker if needed.

If the backing is hard to peel.

Check the photo instructions. When using a blade, work on a flat surface and take care not to injure your hands or damage the product.

Step 01

Cut the length needed

Leave a little extra around the application area for a neat finish.

Step 02

Lightly score the surface

Make a very shallow score where separation is needed. Do not cut through the product or cut while holding it in your hand.

Step 03

Gently separate the layers

Starting at the scored area, gently open and separate with both hands.

Responses may vary by dog.

Response depends on bitter-taste sensitivity, chewing habits, contact point and environment. BitterCare supports behavior management. Observe and adjust placement at first.
